How we work

  1. Consultation

    We look at your business, founders and why you need the AIFC or Astana Hub.

  2. Eligibility check

    We compare your business with the requirements and say so if the route does not fit.

  3. Documents

    We prepare incorporation documents, a business plan and other materials.

  4. Filing

    We file through the official portal and answer the reviewers’ requests.

  5. Ongoing support

    After the decision we help with contracts and corporate matters.
